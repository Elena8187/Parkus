01Who We Are

This website, parkus.net, is operated by Parking Solutions, LLC (“PARKUS,” “we,” “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

License plate recognition at parking locations is covered by a separate policy: our ALPR/LPR Privacy & Usage Policy.

02Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you submit the property form, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide it
  • Property type, property address, and any details you add in the message field
  • Your consent choices and the date and time you submitted the form

If you call or email us, we receive the information you choose to share.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting and font providers may automatically receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the website and keep it secure.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not use third-party tracking tools.

03How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and evaluate your property for a parking program
  • Contact you by email about your property
  • Call or text you, only if you agreed to calls and text messages
  • Keep records of inquiries and consents
  • Protect the website against spam and misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

04Calls and Text Messages

Agreeing to calls and text messages is optional and is not a condition of submitting the form. If you agree, we may contact you at the number you provided about your property inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, or by contacting us.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or text messaging consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

05How We Share Information

We share personal information only as needed to run this website and respond to you:

  • Service providers that help us operate the website and deliver your inquiry, such as our website hosting provider, our form delivery service and our email provider
  • Partners that help us evaluate or operate a parking program for your property, when needed to respond to your request
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect our rights, our users or others
  • Business transfers, if our business is merged, sold or reorganized

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

06How Long We Keep It

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain our ordinary business records, and then delete it.

07How We Protect It

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. This website uses a secure (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. You can also ask us to stop contacting you by email, phone or text.

California residents. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

09Do Not Track

Some browsers send “Do Not Track” signals. Because this website does not track visitors over time or across other websites, and does not allow third parties to do so, it does not change its behavior in response to these signals.

10Children

This website is intended for property owners and businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date above.
