    Scheduled visits to check parking activity and property conditions.

FAQ & next step

Questions owners ask first.

Do I need to invest upfront?

The PARKUS program is offered with no upfront investment from the property owner under an agreed site plan. We assess the property’s requirements before agreeing on the setup and operating arrangements.

How does the revenue share work?

PARKUS operates the parking program and shares parking revenue with you. The owner’s share typically ranges from 65% to 80%, depending on the property, demand and the services included. The agreement defines how revenue is calculated and how any fees or deductions are handled.

Can you lease my land?

PARKUS can consider leasing suitable land or parking space. Rental payments, the lease period and the operating plan are agreed individually.

Can my tenants keep using the lot?

Operating hours and access arrangements are agreed around the property’s existing use. Suitable sites may offer paid parking after business hours or during other approved periods.

What technology does PARKUS use?

PARKUS sites run on the platform of our technology partner Parqour: license plate recognition, QR and app payments, and real-time occupancy and revenue reporting. Equipment and setup for each property are agreed in the site plan.

Will someone physically check my property?

Yes. Scheduled mobile patrol and on-site support can be included in your parking program, provided through Elcoin Security and [ hospitality parking group ]. We agree on visit frequency, operating hours, reporting and escalation procedures before launch. Additional staffing can also be arranged for busy periods and events.

Who handles payments and parking oversight?

PARKUS coordinates mobile payments, parking records and reporting through the Parqour platform. Mobile patrol and on-site enforcement are provided through Elcoin Security, under the scope agreed for each property.

What happens after I submit my property?

We review the details and contact you to discuss the property’s suitability and possible next steps.
